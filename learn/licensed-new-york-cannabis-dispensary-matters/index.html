Why Shopping at a Licensed New York Dispensary Matters

Learn how licensed retail fits New York's regulated adult-use marketplace and what customers should still verify on labels and current state guidance.

What a license tells you

New York's adult-use cannabis marketplace is regulated by the state. Licensed retailers operate under requirements that cover adult-only sales and the handling and sale of regulated products. Choosing a licensed store helps customers distinguish the regulated marketplace from unverified sellers whose products and claims may not meet state requirements.

Elevated Dreams LLC is a New York State licensed adult-use dispensary, License No. OCM-CAURD-26-000353, serving adults 21 and older in Newburgh. Customers should still review each package and follow current state rules; a retailer license is not a substitute for reading product information.

Labels are part of informed shopping

Regulated product packaging can provide product identity, ingredients, cannabinoid amounts, warnings, and other required information. Read the package for the item you are considering rather than relying on a social post, third-party listing, or old photograph. Labels and rules may change over time.

Pay attention to the format and units. THC for flower may be displayed as a percentage; an edible may list milligrams per serving and per package. These measurements are not interchangeable. A higher THC figure is not a quality rating and does not guarantee a specific experience.

Age and responsible-use rules

Adult-use purchases are limited to people 21 and older. Bring valid government-issued photo identification and expect an age check. New York rules also address possession, public consumption, and where cannabis use is allowed. Requirements can change, so consult the New York Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) for current guidance rather than relying on an old summary.

Never drive or operate machinery after consuming cannabis. Keep products in their original packaging and securely stored away from children and pets. Avoid combining cannabis with alcohol or other intoxicating substances. These are important precautions regardless of product format.

Verify claims and ask questions

Licensed retail staff can help explain product categories, label fields, and the store's purchasing process. They cannot guarantee how a product will affect an individual or provide medical advice. Be cautious about any claim that a cannabis product will treat, cure, or prevent a health condition; product descriptions should not be used as a substitute for professional care.

If you see confusing or concerning information, compare the package with current state resources and ask the retailer for clarification. For health questions, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

Check current information before a visit

The live Elevated Dreams menu shows current listings, but selection and availability may vary. Store information, hours, and fulfillment options should also be checked on the current location page. Do not assume that a product, price, or service mentioned elsewhere is still available.

Before you buy

  • Confirm the seller is a licensed New York adult-use retailer.
  • Bring valid identification and meet the 21+ age requirement.
  • Read the product label, including warnings and serving information.
  • Check today's menu and current store details.
  • Review current OCM guidance if you have a legal-rule question.

Regulated retail is one part of responsible shopping. Read the label, follow the directions, and make decisions within current New York rules.
