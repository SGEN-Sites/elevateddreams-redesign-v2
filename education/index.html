01

Cannabis Product Types

Cannabis products fall into several general categories: flower (the dried plant), pre-rolls (ready-to-use), edibles (food and beverages), vapes (oil cartridges and pens), concentrates (high-potency extracts), tinctures and CBD products. Each type has different onset times, durations, and potency levels. Understanding the basics helps you make informed choices.

02

Responsible Use

Start with a low dose and go slow, especially with edibles and concentrates. Effects vary by individual and method of consumption. Never drive or operate heavy machinery after using cannabis. Keep products away from children and pets. Avoid use if pregnant, nursing, or taking medications that may interact.

03

Safe Storage

Store cannabis products in a secure, cool, dark location, out of reach of children and pets. Use child-resistant containers. Keep edibles in their original labeled packaging to avoid confusion with regular food. Proper storage preserves quality and prevents accidental access.

04

Understanding THC and CBD

THC (tetrahydrocannabinol) is the primary intoxicating compound in cannabis. CBD (cannabidiol) is non-intoxicating and is found in both cannabis and hemp. Products vary widely in their THC and CBD content. Always review the product label and ask our staff if you have questions about potency.

05

Edibles and Onset Time

Unlike inhaled products, edibles are absorbed through the digestive system and can take 30 minutes to 2 hours to produce effects. Effects may also last significantly longer. It is important to wait before consuming more. Starting with a low dose is strongly recommended, especially for first-time edible consumers.

06

New York Adult-Use Rules

In New York State, adults 21 and older may legally purchase and possess cannabis from licensed retailers. It is illegal to consume cannabis in public, in motor vehicles, or anywhere smoking is prohibited. Adults may possess up to 3 ounces of cannabis flower and 24 grams of concentrated cannabis. Home cultivation may be permitted under state rules, consult current NYS OCM guidelines.

07

What to Bring to the Store

All customers must present a valid, government-issued photo ID confirming they are 21 years of age or older. Accepted forms include a driver's license, state ID, or passport. Expired identification is not accepted. You may also want to browse the live menu online before visiting to plan your purchase.
