What concentrates are

Concentrates are extracted products and may have a higher concentration of cannabinoids. They have their own package directions and measures, so read the label for the item you are considering.

Start with a low dose and go slow, especially with edibles and concentrates. Effects vary by individual and method of consumption.

What to compare on the label

A higher THC value is not an objective quality score, and a concentrated product is not automatically a better product. Compare like with like.

  • The unit used: percentage, milligrams per serving or total package
  • Other listed cannabinoids
  • Ingredients, warnings and directions
  • Whether the product is comparable to the other item you are considering

New York possession limits

In New York State, adults 21 and older may possess up to 24 grams of concentrated cannabis and up to 3 ounces of cannabis flower.
