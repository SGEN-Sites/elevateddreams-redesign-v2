Where is Elevated Dreams LLC located?

We are at 190 South Plank Road, Unit 10, Newburgh, New York 12550. Call 845-245-3716 with any questions before your visit.

What are your store hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

What do I need to bring?

A valid, government-issued photo ID confirming you are 21 or older, such as a driver's license, state ID or passport. Expired identification is not accepted.

Can I order online for pickup?

Yes. Place your order through our online storefront and select pickup at checkout. Bring a valid ID when you arrive.

Yes, delivery is now available. Orders are placed through our secure online store, where live products, pricing and discounts are managed.

Current offers, such as the Edible Weekend Deal with 20% off all edibles every Friday and Saturday, are on our deals page. Offers may change, so check the live store for current availability, eligibility and final pricing.

Yes. Elevated Dreams LLC holds New York State cannabis license OCM-CAURD-26-000353 and serves adults 21 and older.
